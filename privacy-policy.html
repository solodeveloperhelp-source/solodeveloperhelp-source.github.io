AIBass Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-02

AIBass is an offline music player for local playback of music and supported media files stored on the user's device. The main playback, equalizer, playlists, favorites, sleep timer and app settings work locally on the device.

1. Information the App Uses

AIBass does not require a user account and does not upload the user's local music files to developer-owned servers.

To provide music playback and library features, the app may access local audio files, supported media files and related metadata available through Android media storage, such as file name, artist, album, duration and file location on the device.

The app may also store local app data on the device, including playlists, favorites, equalizer settings, playback settings, sleep timer settings and other preferences needed for the player to work.

2. What the App Does Not Do

AIBass does not provide a music catalog, does not distribute music content and does not claim ownership of the user's local audio files.

AIBass does not intentionally upload the user's local music library to developer-owned servers and does not use cloud synchronization for the user's music files.

3. Android Permissions

Depending on the Android version and device, AIBass may request permissions required for normal player operation.

READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE may be used to find and play local music files stored on the device.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK may be used to keep music playback active in the background and show media playback controls.

MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS may be used for audio output, equalizer and playback-related audio control.

INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE may be used by advertising, consent, billing or related SDK services.

Advertising ID permission may be used by advertising SDKs if available on the device and allowed by the user and Google Play services.

4. Advertising and Consent

AIBass may contain advertising. The app may use Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform and AppLovin MAX advertising technologies.

Advertising SDKs may process data needed to load, show, measure and protect ads, including device identifiers, Advertising ID when available, IP address, app interactions, approximate location derived from network information, diagnostics and other technical data.

In regions where consent is required, the user may be shown a consent request before personalized advertising is used. The availability and content of the consent request may depend on the user's region and applicable requirements.

The user can manage advertising-related choices through the consent dialog where available, Google settings, device settings or other privacy controls provided by the operating system and advertising services.

5. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

AIBass may offer an optional subscription that disables advertising blocks while the subscription is active.

Payments and subscription management are processed by Google Play. AIBass does not receive or store the user's full payment card number or bank details.

The app may receive purchase status information from Google Play Billing to verify whether the subscription is active and to enable or disable advertising-related features accordingly.

6. Data Sharing

AIBass does not sell the user's personal data.

Some data may be processed by third-party service providers used in the app, including advertising, consent, billing and platform services. These providers process data according to their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

The user may also choose to share information manually, for example by using the Android system share function or by contacting support through an email app. In that case, the selected external app or service processes the shared data under its own terms and privacy policy.

7. Data Storage and Deletion

AIBass stores app settings and local library-related data on the user's device.

The user can delete local app data through Android app settings, by clearing the app data, or by uninstalling the app. Local music files are controlled by the user and are not intentionally deleted by AIBass unless the user uses a system or app function that explicitly removes files.

8. Children's Privacy

AIBass is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information through the app, they can contact us using the email address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app functionality, data practices, advertising integrations, billing features or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page.
