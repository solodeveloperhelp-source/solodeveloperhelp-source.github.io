AIBass Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-12

AIBass is an offline music player. The app is designed to play audio files stored on your device and process playback locally on your device.

Information We Collect

AIBass does not create user accounts and does not intentionally collect personal data on external servers.

The app may access audio files and related media metadata stored on your device only to provide playback and library features.

Permissions

AIBass may request access to audio files on your device so it can find and play your music library. If you choose specific files or folders, the app uses that access only for playback and library browsing.

How Data Is Used

Audio metadata, playlists, favorites, and player settings are used locally on your device to operate the app.

Data Sharing

AIBass does not sell your personal data and does not share personal data with third parties for advertising.

Data Storage

App settings and local library-related data may be stored on your device. Removing the app may delete locally stored app data, subject to your device settings.

Children's Privacy

AIBass is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this page.
