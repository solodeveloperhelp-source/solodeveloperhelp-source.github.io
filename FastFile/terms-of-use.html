Users are responsible for selecting the correct files and folders, backing up important data, and the consequences of deletion, moving, renaming, conversion, and other operations. Confirming an operation does not replace checking the selection or making backups.

Opening an image or video depends on the available Android decoder, codec and file condition. A file extension alone does not guarantee that it can be opened. Archiving video and audio preserves the original bytes and may barely reduce size; extract a compressed file before playback. Keep the HiddenVault password and independent backups: uninstalling removes the internal vault and the developer cannot recover a forgotten password.

Faster packing modes may produce a larger archive while preserving the original bytes. Actual speed and compression ratio depend on the format, file type and device.

Unused cached files have a shared budget of 20 MiB and are cleaned automatically. Active working copies, files handed to other apps, and data needed for recovery may temporarily use more space. They are protected while in use; unnecessary temporary files are removed after release, and recovery copies are kept until recovery can safely finish.

5. Trash

When trash is enabled, FastFile may move deleted files to .fastfile_trash. If trash is disabled, emptied, or a file is permanently deleted, recovery through the app may be impossible.
